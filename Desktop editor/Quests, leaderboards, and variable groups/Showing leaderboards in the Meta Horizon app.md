# [Showing leaderboards in the Meta Horizon app](#showing-leaderboards-in-the-meta-horizon-app)

You can use the Desktop Editor to select one leaderboard per world to be visible in the Meta Horizon app.

![](../../_assets/images/bb7ebbb6d3be7de270c5e89e26e1b31bc2fbe11a5e6d301c65daa02c77f6f9cb.png)

## [Sharing an existing leaderboard to Meta Horizon app](#sharing-an-existing-leaderboard-to-meta-horizon-app)

1. Click the **Systems** dropdown in the top bar.
2. Click on **Leaderboards**.
3. Click on the pencil icon next to the leaderboard you want to share.
4. Click on the **Set as default** toggle.
5. Click **Save**.

> [!Note]
>
> If a default leaderboard already existed, it won’t be the default anymore.

![](../../_assets/images/bb5f88b5aed9cd017d18cb706a803dfc4458c375fe898be383cc213b39627dae.png)

## [Creating a leaderboard and sharing to Meta Horizon app](#creating-a-leaderboard-and-sharing-to-meta-horizon-app)

1. Click the **Systems** dropdown in the top bar.
2. Click on **Leaderboards**.
3. Click on the plus icon to create a new leaderboard.
4. Fill in the information for your leaderboard.
5. Click on the **Set as default** toggle.
6. Click **Save**.

> [!Note]
>
> If a default leaderboard already existed, it won’t be the default anymore.

![](../../_assets/images/b9184b53dad37f265085b6cdfb35469af8430c722cdcdff1a505df1e35a98e4b.png)
