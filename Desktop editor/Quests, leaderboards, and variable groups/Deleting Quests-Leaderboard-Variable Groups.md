# [Deleting Quests/Leaderboard/Variable Groups](#deleting-questsleaderboardvariable-groups)

You can use the Desktop Editor to view, create, edit, delete, debug, sort, and search quests, leaderboards, and variable groups, just like you can in VR.

![](../../.assets/images/bb7ebbb6d3be7de270c5e89e26e1b31bc2fbe11a5e6d301c65daa02c77f6f9cb.png)

## [Getting Started](#getting-started)

The first step for using all procedures in this article is to open the **systems panel**:

1. Click the **Systems** dropdown in the top bar.
2. Select the feature you want to modify.

## [How to delete quests](#how-to-delete-quests)

1. Hover over the quest that you want to delete.
2. Click the delete icon. ![](../../.assets/images/d95734469ade898ec145bc4661a11171604f7dace10ab6942fed98653f67e1b3.png)
3. Click ‘Delete’ on the modal dialog that opens.

## [How to delete leaderboards](#how-to-delete-leaderboards)

1. Hover over the leaderboard that you want to delete.
2. Click the delete icon. ![](../../.assets/images/fbfebcb0e9f93020e4340ecc93a3b34f885f6cd6f5a1879b2cad51d51805d16e.png)
3. Click ‘Delete’ on the modal dialog that opens.

## [How to delete variable groups](#how-to-delete-variable-groups)

1. Hover over the variable group that you want to delete. ![](../../.assets/images/0dab0aa972e7711b426bd228e7a7f07e4fcf098912cc20b87f14415988b17c03.png)
2. For variable groups you will need to hover over the variable group, click on the overflow menu, and then select “Delete”.
3. Click ‘Delete’ on the modal dialog that opens.
