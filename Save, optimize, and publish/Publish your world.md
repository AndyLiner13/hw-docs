# [Publish your world](#publish-your-world)

## [Publish your world using the Developer Dashboard](#publish-your-world-using-the-developer-dashboard)

As a world builder, using the [Developer Dashboard](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/manage/worlds) unlocks new functionality for you and your team. You can use the Developer Dashboard to publish your world, create a store page, create and manage assets, manage payments for you and your team, access user and performance analytics, and more.

### [Prerequisites](#prerequisites)

Before you can publish your world using the Developer Dashboard, you must:

- Have a Meta account and set up a development team. For more information, see [Manage your team](Manage%20your%20team.md).

### [Uploading your build](#uploading-your-build)

Whenever you create a new world in the Horizon Desktop Editor, that world will be available on the worlds page of the [Developer Dashboard](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/manage/worlds). As you work on your worlds in Horizon Studio, they will be automatically updated in the Developer Dashboard as well.

### [Publishing your world](#publishing-your-world)

To publish your world using the Developer Dashboard:

1. Open the [Developer Dashboard](https://developers.meta.com/horizon/manage/worlds).
2. Select the world you want to publish.
3. From the **World product detail page** of your world, click **Continue submission**.
4. Add details and images for your world. For more details, see [Add world details](https://developers.meta.com/horizon-worlds/learn/documentation/save-optimize-and-publish/intro-to-worlds-discoveryadd-world-details-in-horizon).
5. When you’re ready to publish, click **Publish**.
